I am a first-year Master’s student at Yale University, with a Biostatistics major in Data Science track. My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, natural language processing, and statistical modelling for all data-driven problems. I completed my undergraduate in Computer Science and Statistics at the University of Toronto. Between my third year and last year of undergrads, I took a gap year roles at Huawei Technologies Canada, where I contributed to cloud data engineering and large-scale data processing systems. I am passionate about advancing computational methods in data science and making impactful contributions through research.
